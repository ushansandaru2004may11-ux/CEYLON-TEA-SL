The Legacy of Pure Ceylon Tea

From the mist-shrouded mountain peaks of Nuwara Eliya to your teacup, discover how Sri Lanka became the world’s most celebrated tea nation.

Chapter 01

Where Mountains Touch The Sky

In 1867, a Scotsman named James Taylor planted the first 19 acres of tea in Loolkondera, Kandy. The unique tropical climate, combined with high elevation mountain air, nutrient-rich soil, and rainfall patterns, created tea leaves with an unmatched aroma and brisk flavor.

Today, CEYLON TEA SL preserves this ancient craftsmanship. We work directly with master plunkers who harvest only the top two leaves and a bud at break of dawn when the dew is still fresh.

Our Craftsmanship

From Estate Leaf to Teacup

01

Hand Plucking

Only tender young tea buds are selectively hand-picked at sunrise by experienced estate artisans.

02

Withering & Rolling

Leaves are gently laid on woven troughs to reduce moisture, then rolled to release essential tea oils.

03

Oxidation & Drying

Natural fermentation develops the signature golden-red liquor hue and crisp aroma before firing.

04

Master Tasting

Expert tea sommelier taste-checks each batch before seal packing into our signature foil tins.
